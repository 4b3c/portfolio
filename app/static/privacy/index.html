Privacy policy: Rocks2WiFi uploader

Last updated November 8, 2026.

The Rocks2WiFi uploader is a private tool Abram Pierce uses to publish videos to his own YouTube channel, Rocks2WiFi. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, it uploads videos, sets their thumbnails and titles, and reads view statistics for the Rocks2WiFi channel. It does not read anyone else's account, and it does not collect personal information about viewers.

What it stores

One sign-in key for the Rocks2WiFi channel, kept on Abram's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone other than Google. The key can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions go through the contact form on abrampierce.com.
