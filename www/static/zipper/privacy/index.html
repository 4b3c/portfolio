Privacy policy: Zipper

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Zipper is a personal assistant Abram Pierce runs for himself and a few friends. Each person gets their own zipper: a private folder of notes about their work and life, kept on Abram's server, that they talk to through Discord. It is not a commercial product and is offered only to people Abram invites.

What it accesses

If you connect Google, your zipper can read your Gmail messages and create events in a calendar of its own in your Google Calendar. It cannot send email or see your other calendars. If you choose to have it sort your mail into labels, it can also move messages between labels; it still never sends anything. It only reads mail to keep your own notes and task list current: deadlines, forms and replies you owe someone.

What it stores, and where

One sign-in key for your Google account, stored only in your own zipper on Abram's server. Your zipper keeps notes and tasks drawn from your mail (a short summary and who it was from), not copies of messages. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

To work out what an email means for your notes, your zipper passes its text to Claude, the AI model made by Anthropic that runs your zipper, under your own Claude account. Mail is not used to train any model.

Google API data

Zipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing it

You can cut off access at any time from Google account permissions. Ask Abram and your zipper, with everything in it, is deleted.
